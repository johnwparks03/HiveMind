Project Falcon: budget memo

Project Falcon is the internal code name for the Harborview office move. This memo records the budget
decision so the team has one place to look it up.

Decision: the approved Falcon budget is $48,200. It was approved on 14 September 2026 by the
facilities committee and covers furniture, cabling and two weekends of mover labour.

Not included in the $48,200: the lease deposit, which is handled separately by finance, and any
equipment bought for the new kitchen.

The project lead is the facilities manager. Questions about the Falcon budget go to the facilities
inbox, not to the committee.
